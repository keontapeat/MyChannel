## 🚀 WHAT IS MYCHANNEL?

MyChannel is an AI-powered video platform where creators can:
- **Upload & Earn**: 90% revenue split (vs YouTube's 55%)
- **Compete**: Real money VS matches ($1-$100K wagers)
- **Get Coached**: AI-powered viral predictions & creator tips
- **Go Live**: Stream with real-time chat & awards
- **Rank Up**: Win championship medals across 6 divisions

## 🤖 AI FEATURES TO TEST:

### 1. **AI Creator Studio** (⭐ PRIORITY)
**Location**: Creator Studio → AI Studio tab

Test these features:

**Location**: Gaming tab → VS Matches → Create Match

Steps:
1. Tap "Create Match"

**What to look for:**

- Are all options clear?

- Can you track match status?

### **Championship Medals**
**Location**: Gaming tab → Championship Medals

**What to look for:**
- Can you see all 6 medal divisions?
- Are rankings displayed correctly?
- Can you view top competitors?
- Is your own ranking shown?

## 🐛 REPORTING BUGS:

Found a bug? We want to know! Here's how to report it:

### **In-App (Recommended)**:
1. Settings → Send Feedback
2. Or shake your device → Send Feedback

### **Email**:
bugs@mychannel.live

### **What to Include**:
- **What happened**: Describe the issue
- **What you expected**: What should have happened
- **Steps to reproduce**: How to trigger the bug
- **Device**: iPhone model & iOS version
- **Screenshots/Video**: If possible

### **Example Good Bug Report**:
```
ISSUE: Video upload fails for large files
EXPECTED: Upload should work for files up to 500MB
STEPS: 1) Tap Upload 2) Select 200MB video 3) Fill metadata 4) Tap Publish
RESULT: Upload fails at 50% with error message
DEVICE: iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.1
ATTACHED: Screenshot of error
```

## ⚠️ KNOWN ISSUES (Don't Report These):

### Minor Issues We're Aware Of:
- Profile banner upload may be slow on poor connection
- Some animations may stutter on iPhone X or older
- Search suggestions limited to 10 results
- Live stream preview quality auto-adjusts
- Mini-player occasionally shows black screen (restart app to fix)

### Coming in Next Build:
- Picture-in-Picture optimization
- More AI agent integrations (20 more!)
- Advanced analytics dashboard
- Tournament brackets
- Live shopping features

### Workarounds:
- **Upload fails**: Try smaller file (<100MB) or better WiFi
- **Playback stutters**: Lower quality in settings
- **App crashes**: Force quit and restart
- **Stuck loading**: Log out and log back in
