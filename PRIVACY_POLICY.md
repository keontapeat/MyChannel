# Privacy Policy for MyChannel

**Last Updated**: November 14, 2024  
**Effective Date**: November 14, 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to MyChannel ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Username, email address, password (encrypted), date of birth
- **Profile Information**: Display name, bio, profile picture, banner image
- **Content**: Videos, thumbnails, comments, live streams, descriptions, titles, tags
- **Payment Information**: Payment method details (processed securely through Stripe)
- **Communications**: Messages to support, feedback, bug reports

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: Videos watched, search queries, likes, comments, subscriptions
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, browser type, access times, pages viewed
- **Location Data**: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS)
- **Performance Data**: App crashes, errors, performance metrics

### AI-Generated Data
- **AI Insights**: Viral predictions, creator coaching recommendations, analytics insights
- **Personalization**: AI-generated content recommendations based on your viewing history
- **Support Interactions**: Conversations with our AI support agent

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### Provide and Improve Services
- Create and manage your account
- Process video uploads and streaming
- Enable VS match participation and payments
- Provide personalized content recommendations
- Generate AI-powered analytics and insights
- Offer 24/7 AI support assistance

### Communication
- Send important service updates
- Respond to your inquiries

- Provide marketing communications (with your consent)

### Safety and Security
- Detect and prevent fraud using AI fraud detection
- Moderate content for policy violations
- Protect against abuse and spam
- Verify age for age-restricted features
- Secure payment transactions

### Analytics and Research
- Analyze usage patterns to improve the app
- Develop new features and services
- Generate anonymous, aggregated statistics
- Train and improve AI models

### Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Resolve disputes
- Protect our rights and property

## Your Data Choices and Rights

You have the right to:

### Access and Control
- **Access**: View your personal data
- **Update**: Edit your profile and account information
- **Delete**: Request deletion of your account and data
- **Download**: Export your data in a portable format

### Privacy Controls
- **Public/Private**: Control who can see your content
- **Notifications**: Manage notification preferences
- **Data Collection**: Opt out of certain data collection (may limit features)
- **Marketing**: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

### How to Exercise Rights
- **In-App**: Settings → Privacy & Data
- **Email**: privacy@mychannel.live
- **Web**: Visit mychannel.live/privacy

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
- **Authentication**: Secure login with optional two-factor authentication
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access to personal data
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and penetration testing
- **AI Security**: Fraud detection to protect your payments

**Note**: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide our services
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our agreements

**Account Deletion**: When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain it for legal compliance or fraud prevention.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MyChannel is NOT intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

- **Age Verification**: Users must confirm they are 13+ during sign-up
- **Parental Consent**: Users 13-17 should have parental permission
- **Kids Content**: Special protections for content marked "Made for Kids"
- **Reporting**: If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us immediately

## International Data Transfers

- **Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

Contact us at privacy@mychannel.live to exercise these rights.

## European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EU/EEA residents have additional rights:
- **Data Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Right to Object**: Object to certain processing of your data
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for processing
- **Lodge Complaint**: File complaint with supervisory authority

Our lawful basis for processing includes:
- **Contract**: To provide services you requested
- **Legitimate Interest**: To improve and secure our services
- **Consent**: For marketing and certain data uses
- **Legal Obligation**: To comply with laws
